# What forgetful people actually struggle with (research, 23 Nov 2026)

## How this was done (and its limits)
- Reddit and Twitter/X could not be reached from my research environment (their sites and APIs are blocked there), so I could not pull Reddit threads directly.
- Instead I read **27 pages with 562 user-written posts, comments, reviews and tips**: forum threads (Tildes, Lemmy, Discourse, Straight Dope, Beyond Blue, Blind, Mayo Clinic Connect), Hacker News and LessWrong threads, Trustpilot reviews of the Limitless and Bee AI wearables, an app review, and two compilations of Reddit/ADHD tips (22 Reddit tips via AOL/Bored Panda; 131 user tips via Beat ADHD, paraphrased by its author). Plus 5 editorial articles for market facts.
- Each post was tagged by problem (a post can have several). About 275 posts were off-topic (general ADHD lifestyle advice, jokes, support replies) and are left out of the ranking.
- Dates range from 2011 to Nov 2026; most of the strongest signals are 2023–2026. Tagging is judgment-based (±2 per tag). Treat the numbers as directional, not a survey.

Grouped the way Kai would solve them:
- **Right thing at the right moment** (ranks 1, 6, 7): ~121 posts. The single biggest need.
- **Capture fast and get it back later** (ranks 2, 8): ~67 posts.
- **Where is my stuff / did I do it** (ranks 3, 10): ~42 posts.

## What people do today (what works)
Phone alarms/reminders/calendar (~49), paper notes and whiteboards (~41), notes apps and plain-text search (~58), a fixed "home" for objects or putting keys on the thing to take (~26), physical cues on the door (~13), texting/emailing themselves (~10), mnemonics or saying it aloud (~9).

Key lesson: people's best tricks are **cues at the moment of need** (keys on the bag, note on the door) and **zero-effort capture**. Apps fail when they add friction or when notifications get swiped away and vanish.

## Market facts that matter
- **Google has removed location reminders three times**: Assistant location reminders (2022), Keep reminders moved to Google Tasks without location (Oct 2025, Google: "You can no longer create or get location-based reminders"), and Gemini, which replaced Assistant on Android from Sept 2026, can't set them. Users are publicly angry about it (Android Police, Aug 2026; How-To Geek, Jan 2026). Samsung's Reminder app still has them; Tasks.org and Todoist have location features too. **This is an open gap on Android, right now.**
- **AI wearables are struggling**: Limitless reviewers report the service shutting down after its acquisition with "no offline or basic usage option"; Bee reviews are mostly about dead devices and support, and reviewers worry about data. TechCrunch (May 2026): "intrigued and slightly creeped out". Lesson: people want the memory, but not always-on recording of everyone, and not a cloud that can disappear. Kai's on-device design is a selling point.
- Vendor claim (unverified): most "second brain" systems are abandoned within 90 days because capture is too much work.
